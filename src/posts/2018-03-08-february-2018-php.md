<h2>Stand up against the standup</h2>
At work, we're currently taking an in-depth look at our processes and how we can improve them. As much as I like getting together with my colleagues on a daily basis to hear what everybody is working on, the daily standup can be quite a productivity killer if done (and planned) wrong. To solve this issue I suggested trying doing asynchronous standups on Slack inspired by articles like <a href="http://social.techcrunch.com/2016/08/06/stand-up-against-the-stand-up/">Stand up against the stand-up</a>.
