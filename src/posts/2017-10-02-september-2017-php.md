Another month is coming to an end, and again that means I'll try to summarize some of the most interesting articles I've read. Because of holidays, it's been a pretty short month for me work-wise. That also means that this month's list is pretty short, but the content is really high quality.

For a while, I've been considering how to prevent my controllers to get too bloated. Laravel 5.5 introduces <a href="https://laravel-news.com/laravel-5-5-responsable">the responsable interface</a>, which I believe will help provide a nice convention for where to put the logic that often ends up in the controllers. Another option suggested by Jens Segers, is to turn the normal entity-based controllers into callable <a href="https://jenssegers.com/85/goodbye-controllers-hello-request-handlers">request handlers</a>, narrowing their focus, which I also think is an interesting approach.

I first heard about Event Sourcing at last year's <a href="https://laracon.eu/2016/">Laracon EU</a> at Mitchell van Wijngaarden's talk <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kq3wgGRsVvs">Future is a thing of the past</a>. Mitchell's background was very theoretic, though, meaning that at the time he hadn't built any large-scale applications using the approach. That's why I found it really interesting when Barry O Sullivan wrote his article <a href="https://dev.to/barryosull/event-sourcing-what-it-is-and-why-its-awesome">Event Sourcing: What it is and why it's awesome</a>, providing an introduction to the concept of event sourcing, from somebody who works with it in the trenches of every day development.

I also found this list of <a href="https://vuejsdevelopers.com/2017/09/11/vue-js-vuex-plugins/">5 interesting Vuex plugins</a>, for the Vue state management plugin Vuex.
